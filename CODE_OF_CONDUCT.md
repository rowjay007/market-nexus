# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Standards

Examples of positive behavior:

- Being respectful and constructive
- Accepting feedback gracefully
- Focusing on the best outcome for the project

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, discrimination, or abusive language
- Personal attacks or trolling
- Publishing private information without consent

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, and contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.
